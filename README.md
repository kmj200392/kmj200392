# Hi, I'm Minjae Kim

**AI researcher & developer** interested in building capable, efficient, and adaptive language models and systems.

I'm a research intern at [KAIST MLAI Lab](https://www.mlai-kaist.com/), advised by [Prof. Sung Ju Hwang](https://www.sungjuhwang.com/), and study Computer Science and Engineering at Korea University. I enjoy turning research ideas into tools and experiences people can use.

[Website](https://minjaek.im/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=acKBQrMAAAAJ) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kmj200392) · [Email](mailto:minjae_kim@kaist.ac.kr)

## Research interests

- **LLM agents:** reasoning and acting over long horizons with efficient token and compute use.
- **Memory and knowledge:** integrating and compressing information for long-running agents.
- **Efficient adaptation:** exploring parametric mechanisms for adaptable language models.
